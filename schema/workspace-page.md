Claude's workspace. Everything Claude writes to Notion lives here, filed by the `notion-workspace` plugin. Things flow from left to right: a lead you want to look into, notes once you do, what you've learned across them, your own learnings and insights in your own words, and what you build.

## How it's organised

- **To explore**: leads that caught your eye but aren't worth a deep dive yet: ideas, books, articles, tools. Each has one line on what it is and a status (New, Done or Dropped). Go through them in the *To review* view. When you dig into one, its notes go in the Library and link back.
- **Library**: one row per thing written up: notes on a video, podcast, article or paper, a recap of a discussion, research, or a curated list such as a watch list. Each row has a type, a one-line takeaway, its topics and any related projects, and shows the learnings and insights that came out of it.
- **Topics**: one page per subject. Each keeps a short running synthesis across every Library item on that subject ("Where I've got to"), plus open questions. Start here to revise a subject.
- **Learnings**: one row per thing you've understood or found insightful, in your own words. A *Learning* is your own conclusion; an *Insight* is a one-line capture of something from a source that struck you. Each has where it came from, tags, and links to the Library item you were going through and to a topic or project. Learnings keep your original message in a toggle underneath. Use the *Learnings* and *Insights* views to look at each kind on its own.
- **Projects**: one page per thing you're building or might build, from a saved idea to a shipped project. Each has a pitch and a stage, and links to the Library items about it. Working documents such as specs and plans are sub-pages of the project.
- **Notes status**: *Notes ready* (written by Claude; stays this way until you've reviewed it), *Reviewed* (you've finished editing; Claude learns from your changes the next time it files something), *Done* (Claude has learned from your edits).

## Ask Claude

- "Add <thing> to explore": saves a lead with a line on what it is.
- "Make notes on <link>": files a Library row and updates the relevant topics.
- "Save this discussion": recaps the conversation into the Library.
- "Add this to my learnings": files what you just explained, in your own words, with your original message kept alongside. Mention the video, article or talk you were going through and it's linked to its Library row.
- "Save this insight": adds a one-line insight from something you read or watched, linked to its Library row.
- "Save this project idea": adds it to Projects, linked to any related notes.
- "What do I know about <topic>?": answers from the Topics page and its sources.
